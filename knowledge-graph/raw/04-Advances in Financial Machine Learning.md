# Advances in Financial Machine Learning

Marcos López de Prado presents financial ML methods, including ways to structure financial data, conduct research, and backtest while addressing false positives. Wiley lists the book as a 2018 publication. [Publisher record](https://uat.store.wiley.com/en-us/advances-in-financial-machine-learning-p-9781119482109)

**Why it matters for PulseQuant:** the document proposes FinBERT and triple-barrier/meta-labeling. This book is relevant to those ideas and to avoiding leakage and overfit in financial ML research.

**Read it after:** a simple baseline, fixed hypothesis, point-in-time data, and reproducible holdout protocol exist. More complex modeling increases researcher degrees of freedom and does not repair a weak or absent signal.

This is an advanced methods book, not evidence that the project's NLP or ML claims work, and not a promise of positive returns.
