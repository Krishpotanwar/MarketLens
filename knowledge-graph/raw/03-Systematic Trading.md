# Systematic Trading

Robert Carver's book covers rule design, fitting, portfolio allocation, forecasts, volatility targeting, position sizing, and trading costs. The publisher lists the first edition as published 14 September 2015 and describes examples across equities and other asset classes. [Publisher record](https://www.harriman-house.com/systematic-trading)

**Why it matters for PulseQuant:** the source mixes forecast probabilities, fixed risk sizing, and execution costs. This book gives a practical framework for keeping forecast, risk, allocation, and implementation decisions distinct.

**Apply it to:** convert a validated forecast into a capped position; size positions against volatility and available capital; evaluate a portfolio of rules after trading costs; avoid repeated optimization on the same historical sample.

The publisher's language about trading “profitably” is marketing, not an empirical guarantee. The book's broad framework also needs adaptation to Indian intraday rules and the chosen broker.
